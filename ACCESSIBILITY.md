# Accessibility

Squire should be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, larger text or high contrast.

## Where it runs

Squire runs in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on iPhone and Android phones, and as a desktop app on Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Known limitations

- Some buttons use emoji as their icon. Each one has a text label for screen readers, but a few may still be missing.
- The photo viewer uses swipe and pinch gestures. The arrow keys and Escape work on a keyboard.
- The QR scanner needs a camera. You can always type a SKU instead.

## Report a barrier

If something is hard or impossible for you to use, please [open an issue](https://github.com/sirgijoe/squire-sack/issues/new/choose) and say what you were trying to do and what got in the way, plus your device and any assistive technology you use. Accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
